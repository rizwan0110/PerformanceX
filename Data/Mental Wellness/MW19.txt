## Depression

Feeling sad or depressed is common and happens to everyone sometimes. If the depression persists for a long time, and you experience more symptoms, you may have developed depression.

Depression is one of the most common mental illnesses in Sweden. Almost a third of the Swedish population suffers from depression at some point in their lives, and elite athletes fall ill to about the same extent as the general population. For example, the world's greatest Olympian of all time, Michael Phelps, has spoken publicly about how he struggled with depression during his career. Hereditary factors, vulnerability, and environmental factors can contribute to the development of depression. Depression can also be triggered by sport-specific factors, such as injuries, career transitions, or overtraining syndrome.

## Symptoms of depression: Two of the most central symptoms of depression are depression and reduced interest in activities that you previously enjoyed. Other common symptoms are fatigue/lack of energy, changes in weight and appetite, sleep problems, anxiety, negative thoughts about oneself, the future and the world around you, low self-esteem, concentration problems, and suicidal thoughts.

## What can you do in case of depression?

There are certain things you can do to feel better. However, we would like to encourage you to seek professional help if you feel that you are suffering from depression.

Do things that you usually enjoy, even if you don't feel like it, as this increases your chance of experiencing positive emotions that can boost your mood. Talk to a loved one about how you feel. Maintain good habits. Try to sleep and eat at regular times and try to get healthy food. Make sure you have some activity booked in the morning, so you have a reason to get up in the morning. Staying outdoors. Daylight is important for well-being, so feel free to take a walk or a bike ride in the middle of the day when it is lightest. Avoid drinking alcohol when you are depressed, it can increase depressive symptoms. Try to find ways to unwind, if you feel that the depression is due to stress. ## When and where should you seek help in case of depression?
If you feel very bad and have thoughts of hurting yourself, or have suicidal thoughts, you should seek emergency care where you are at the moment. If you have experienced several of the above symptoms for more than two weeks and if you have difficulty functioning normally in your everyday life, we recommend that you seek help from healthcare. If you feel slightly depressed, perhaps due to an injury or because you are going through a performance slump, but still do not experience any of the above symptoms, we recommend that you seek support in the form of sports psychology counseling.

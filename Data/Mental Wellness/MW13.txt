## How to Balance between stress and recovery?

One of the biggest challenges that elite athletes face is to continuously balance the training load and the psychosocial loads that exist in life with sufficient recovery. That you recover both physically and mentally from strains is an important prerequisite for good performance, but also for feeling good. It is therefore wise to learn the importance of recovery at an early stage in your career and to develop your own strategies for what it can look like in everyday life. Below you will find some tips on how you can start working with recovery to get a balance in your everyday life and be able to maintain a long and sustainable career.

Make sure you sleep enough and that your sleep is perceived as good. Replenish your energy. Diet is also important for recovery, and it is mainly about getting enough energy. Think about what exactly you think is restorative. Having a list of restorative activities that you can undertake makes recovery more concrete. Book restorative activities. Elite athletes are often quite good at planning training, competitions, travel, work or school assignments, and rest days. But what recovery the rest day should contain, and another recovery, is usually not planned to the same extent. Practice mindfulness. You can get better quality in your restorative activities when you actively try to be present in the moment and don't get caught up in your thoughts. Mindfulness training, where you learn to be here and now, can be a good complement to recovery. But regular mindfulness training not only teaches you to be in the here and now as you recover, but has also been shown to be stress-reducing in its own right.

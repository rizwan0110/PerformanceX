## Exercises to identify values

With the help of the following exercises, you can explore and clarify your values ​​as an athlete. Values ​​are not the same as goals. However, your values ​​can serve as a guide when setting goals. The host can guide you in the moment when you have to make a decision. The host can also be a kind of compass in difficult moments, help in guiding you in the direction that makes sense for you. Values ​​are helpful in increasing motivation for actions that enrich and give meaning to, for example, your sportsmanship.
- Identify host: The exercise aims to help you elicit the values ​​you have for your sportsmanship. Set aside some time (approx. 10 min.), make sure you can be undisturbed, and put your mobile phone on silent. Start the audio file by clicking the link. https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ljudfiler/identifiera-varden.mp3

- Write a newspaper article about yourself: The exercise aims to help you examine what values ​​you have for your sportsmanship. Click on the link https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ovningsblad/ovningsblad_skriv-en-tidningsartikel-om-dig-sjalv.pdf and you will be taken to a pdf. Read the instructions and start reflecting and writing down. You can access the document online or download and print it.

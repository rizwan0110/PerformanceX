## Exercises to increase motivation

The various exercises that deal with motivation and goal setting are below : 
- Learn more about goals : Knowing the different types of goals that exist, as well as knowing what advantages and disadvantages they bring, can be valuable information for you when setting your goals. We recommend that you read through the document below before making a goal setting plan. Click on the link https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ovningsblad/ovningsblad_lar-dig-mer-om-mal.pdf and you will be taken to a pdf. Read the instructions. You can access the document online or you can download and print it.
- Set goals for the competition: Setting concrete goals before a competition is good for increasing motivation, but also for having a clear picture of what you want to achieve. It makes it easier to prepare for the competition, to know what to focus on during the competition, and it provides a good basis for evaluating the competition. With the document below you can get help in the process. Click on the link https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ovningsblad/ovningsblad_satta-upp-mal-for-tavlingen.pdf and you will be taken to a pdf. Read the instructions and fill out the document with your goals. You can access the document online or download and print it.
- Motivation factors: Intrinsic motivation, the most beneficial type of motivation, can increase when we are able to satisfy our three basic psychological needs. Read more about these needs and reflect on whether you can do or change something in your training plan to optimize your inner motivation. Click on the link https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ovningsblad/ovningsblad_motivationsfaktorer.pdf and you will be taken to a pdf. Read the instructions and reflect on the three areas. You can access the document online or download and print it.

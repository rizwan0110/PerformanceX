## Motivation is always directed towards a specific thing, for example your sport. No one is motivated before everything or before nothing, it is rather that we are motivated by different things and activities. Motivation also implies a certain level of effort: If you are highly motivated about an activity, you tend to put a lot of effort into it. Motivation is of course an important ingredient in a sporting venture, but it is not always at its peak during an entire sporting career. You therefore do not need to be stressed or worried if you feel that your motivation is low. Rather, it is normal for it to vary a little from day to day and from season to season, and it can be affected by where you are in the training process. If, on the other hand, you feel that motivation has been lower than usual over a longer period of time, it may be good to try to find out why this is so and what you can do to become more motivated again.

## How can you improve motivation?
- Learn more about and define your values. Have clear values ​​and act in the direction of the values ​​that you find satisfactory and that increase the likelihood of positive reinforcement in everyday life, which boosts motivation and well-being.
- Learn more about and set goals. Focusing on something that you find valuable can feel motivating, and if you succeed in achieving the goal, it can give you a sense of competence, which in turn is motivating.
- Create conditions that feel motivating: If, for example, you notice that you are more motivated and have an easier time going to the gym if you go there with others, try to arrange so that you have a training buddy with you.
- Think about which parts of your sports venture you have difficulty motivating yourself to and if there is any possibility of making changes that could increase your motivation.

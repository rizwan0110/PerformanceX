## Exercises for relaxation and recovery
The primary purpose of relaxation exercises is to give the body and brain rest and reduce tension. It is good for elite athletes to know some relaxation exercises and be able to use them when needed, for example during stressful periods or as part of recovery. Relaxation exercises should be practiced regularly, once you have practiced for a while you can use them in situations where you experience stress. In addition, regular relaxation training leads to many positive bodily changes.

- Progressive Muscle Relaxation: The progressive muscle relaxation exercise is a method that aims to make muscles relax. Emotional stress and worry are usually linked to increased muscle tension and physiological activation. By reducing the muscular tension, the emotional tension and anxiety can also be reduced. The exercise involves first tensing a certain muscle group for about 10 seconds, and then relaxing the muscle group.

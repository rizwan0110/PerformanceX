## Exercises in mindfulness

Mindfulness training primarily aims to train your ability to be present in the present moment. More concretely, mindfulness exercises involve training in different skills, for example being able to note thoughts, feelings and bodily sensations when they appear, being able to keep a distance from thoughts, being able to accept the different experiences that arise in the moment and being able to shift the attention to where it does most benefit. These skills are central if you want to increase your psychological flexibility, which in turn is related to well-being and performance. Mindfulness exercises can be experienced as relaxing, but that is not the primary purpose, rather it is the training of the above-mentioned skills.I do not recommend mindfulness training when you feel very unwell, are in crisis or have just experienced something terrible. In these cases, it is better to seek help.
- Breathing Anchor: In the Breathing Anchor exercise, you practice the skill of being able to keep your attention on one and the same thing (the breath), and being able to note when your attention wanders off to something else. Set aside some time (about 10 min), make sure you can be undisturbed and put your mobile on silent. Start the audio file by clicking the link: https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ljudfiler/andningsankare.mp3
- Body Scan: In this exercise, you practice the skill of being able to shift your attention to different places, and to become better at noting when your attention wanders off to something else. Set aside some time (approx. 10 or 25 min.), make sure you can be undisturbed, and put your mobile phone on silent. Start the audio file by clicking the link : https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ljudfiler/kroppsskanning-lang-variant.mp3
- Mental Note: In the exercise, you practice noting when thoughts, feelings, bodily sensations or sounds catch your attention. You practice naming these experiences and then bringing your attention back to your breathing again. Set aside some time (approx. 10 min.), make sure you can be undisturbed and put your mobile phone on silent. Start the audio file by clicking the link: https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ljudfiler/mental-notering.mp3 
- Mountain Meditation : The exercise focuses on evoking the image of a mountain. The mountain can remind you to remain stable and balanced even in stormy times. The exercise can help you induce a sense of stability and calmness. Set aside some time (approx. 15 min.), make sure you can be undisturbed and put your mobile phone on silent. Start the audio file by clicking the link: https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ljudfiler/bergsmeditation.mp3
- Notice Body: In the exercise, you will practice bringing attention to various stimuli, such as bodily sensations, sounds, breathing, and thoughts. Set aside some time (approx. 5 min.), make sure you can be undisturbed and put your mobile phone on silent. Start the audio file by clicking the link:  https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ljudfiler/lagg-marke-till-kropp.mp3 
- Check-in: The exercise is particularly suitable before starting a new activity, for example when you come home from school/work and are about to start a training session. With the help of the exercise, you can become more present in the moment. Set aside some time (approx. 4 min.), make sure you can be undisturbed and put your mobile phone on silent. Start the audio file by clicking the link: https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ljudfiler/check-in.mp3
- Come back to the present when stressed: This exercise is good when you experience high stress. It can help you anchor yourself in the present, which is often experienced as stress-reducing. Set aside some time (approx. 5 min.), make sure you can be undisturbed and put your mobile phone on silent. Start the audio file by clicking the link: https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ljudfiler/komma-tillbaka-till-nuet-vid-stress.mp3
- Attention shift: In the exercise, you practice shifting your attention with willpower, from things within you to things outside of you. That skill is especially important when you get caught up in something inside, such as thoughts or feelings, and then want to be able to bring your attention to something in your surroundings, such as the match you are playing. The exercise comes in two variants: A variant that refers to attention shift in general and one that is more directed to your training context. The latter is suitable when you are in a training or competition environment. Choose what suits you best. Set aside some time (approx. 3 min.), make sure you can be undisturbed and put your mobile on silent. Start the audio file by clicking on the appropriate link:  https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ljudfiler/uppmarksamhetsskifte-i-traningskontext.mp3

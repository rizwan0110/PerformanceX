## How can you develop or Improve Concentration?
- Think about where you best put your attention during a practice or a competition. Is it a relatively narrow, outer area that you need to focus on (for example, the tennis ball), or is it a more open, wide, outer area that is important (for example, keeping track of teammates and opponents)? Are there times during the competition where it might be good to turn your attention inward, that is, on your body, your feelings or your thoughts?
- Think about what might distract or cause you to lose your focus when playing sports; are they external disturbances (for example, crowd shouts or referee decisions) or are they internal disturbances (for example, thoughts of past mistakes or strong bodily sensations)? Once you have noted some such disturbances, you can move on and think about how you want to act when these disturbances occur. The better you prepare for it, have a plan, and practice the necessary skills, the easier it will be for you to notice the disturbance and bring your attention back to the right place.
- Simulate "disturbances" in your training routine. For example, you may have crowd noise on when you practice, or your coach may call out wrong refereeing decisions that you should try to deal with in a good way.

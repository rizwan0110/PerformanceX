## Exercises in self-compassion

The following two exercises are particularly suitable for moments when you are feeling heavy because of a less successful effort in training, competition/match, or championship. With the help of the exercises, you can get strategies for how you can face yourself in such a situation with kindness, instead of criticizing or judging yourself for how it went. This approach is beneficial for your well-being and at the same time promotes the behavior of learning from what happened and thus being able to develop further in the future.
- Kindness to Yourself After Failure: The practice can help you treat yourself with kindness after a failure. Set aside some time (approx. 15 min.), make sure you can be undisturbed, and put your mobile phone on silent. Start the audio file by clicking the link. https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ljudfiler/vanlighet-med-dig-sjalv-efter-misslyckande.mp3
- To face yourself with kindness in difficult times: In this written exercise, you must describe various aspects of a situation where you face adversity, for example after an unsuccessful competition or match. The purpose of the exercise is for you to be able to treat yourself with kindness in difficult moments. Click on the link https://utbildning.sisuforlag.se/globalassets/rfsisu/publik/idrottspsykologi/ovningsblad/ovningsblad_att-mota-dig-sjalv-med-vanlighet-i-svara-stunder.pdf  and you will be taken to a pdf. Read the instructions and start reflecting and writing down. You can access the document online or download and print it.

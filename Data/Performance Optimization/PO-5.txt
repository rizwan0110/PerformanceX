## Performance Factor
In order to develop your performance and be able to be the best when it matters, it is wise to practice developing your psychological skills. However, developing and optimizing your performance can sometimes also mean that you need to review your life situation and your challenges outside of sport.
What can you do yourself to improve performance?
Here follows a list of various factors that can hinder you in your development and inhibit your performance. Go through the list and think about whether there are any of these factors that you need to look at a little more carefully.
- Sleep is one of our most important sources of recovery and it is fundamental to performance as well as our physical and mental health. Athletes who do not sleep enough for a long time, or sleep poorly, may find it difficult to cope with training or to perform in competition.
- During certain periods in life, it can be difficult to focus on training and disconnect things that happen outside of sports. For example, it could be that you are very worried about your living situation or feel anxiety in social situations and that this takes a lot of your energy.
- You may find that other chores and activities take up too much of your time and attention and interfere with your commitment. An example could be that you spend a lot of time playing (gaming and/or gambling), taking substances or spending a lot of time and energy with your mobile phone and/or on social media.
- If you think a lot about your body and your eating, and/or if you feel that losing weight is a prerequisite for achieving better results, you may be at risk of developing an eating disorder, a serious disruption in your venture.
- Experiencing stress is very common - everyone gets stressed at some point. But if you feel that stress is taking over and making it difficult for you to develop further in your sport and is an obstacle to your performance in competition, we recommend that you try to do something about it.
